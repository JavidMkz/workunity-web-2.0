09 — FAQ

Common questions

01How do I become a WorkUnity partner?
Fill in the form. We carry out a check and an interview, and sign an agreement if the outcome is positive.
02What do I get as a partner?
Access to verified vacancies or candidates, standards, training and a transparent commission model.
03When am I paid?
On the result: once the candidate has arrived and completed the probation period.
04Can I bring both employers and candidates?
Yes — once accredited, you can work in both directions.
