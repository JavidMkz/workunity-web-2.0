09 — FAQ

Common questions

01How much do your services cost?
The first consultation is free. The cost of specific services depends on the situation and is stated before any work begins. We never charge for a "guaranteed result".
02How can I check whether a job offer is genuine?
Do not pay before it is checked. We will verify the employer’s documents free of charge. Use the form on this page or WhatsApp.
03I am not being paid. What should I do?
Tell us what happened. We will assess the situation and set out the options — from negotiation to approaching the state authorities.
04My employer is holding my documents. Is that legal?
Withholding documents without lawful grounds is prohibited. If your passport is being held, contact us immediately.
