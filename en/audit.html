01 — What We Check

What We Check

01

Unified work permit and resident card

Whether they’re still valid, and when they expire.

02

Registration at place of stay

Whether it exists, and whether it’s overdue.

03

Employer match

Whether the employer on paper is the one the person actually works for.

Employment contract

Whether it’s signed, and whether the terms match reality.

Renewal deadlines

02 — The Process

How It Works

01

You submit a request

Name, phone number, site address, how many foreign workers you have.

02

We agree on a time

Usually within 2–3 days.

03

We come to the site

We review the documents. Takes about an hour.

04

You get a written report

What’s fine, where the risk is, and what it costs to fix. Free, no obligation.

We’re not a government body. And we don’t report anything to anyone.

The audit is completely confidential. Our job is to help you get things in order before an inspection — not to report violations. Whatever we see stays between us. If you want, we’ll sign a confidentiality agreement.

04 — What You Get

What You Get

  • The full picture for each foreign worker
  • A list of problems and what each one risks
  • A clear plan: what to fix and what it costs
  • Peace of mind — the inspection won’t catch you off guard
05 — Why It Matters

Expired documents aren’t a small thing.

For the worker: a fine, deportation, an entry ban. For the employer: fines for employing foreign workers without a permit, and the risk of ending up on the Unreliable Employers Register. After that, hiring new workers gets a lot harder. It’s easier to check now than to sort it out after an inspection.

If you are yet to hire, we have set out the procedure step by step in the employer's guide.

06 — About the Money

The audit is free. Fixing violations isn’t.

If we find problems, we’ll offer to fix them: renew the registration, arrange the permit, put the contracts in order. You decide — with us or with anyone else. The report is yours either way.
